# Podcast episode cover art

Produce a square cover art image for the podcast episode with the given title. Whenever possible, produce a vibrant and energetic image. It would also help for it to be uniquely creative. As feasible, use a variety of colors and styles.

Unless explicitly requested:
1. Do not show any text or words or captions whatsoever in the generated image. There should be no labels or letters at all in the generated image. Imagine the image to be a work of art, not to be ruined by the written form. I am not asking for an infographic, so don't make one.
2. Do not show any people in the generated image unless it's specifically called for.
3. Do not use boring or overly simple designs.
4. Do not produce a portrait or landscape ratio image. The requested image is of a square shape, implying a 1:1 ratio, preferably a 1024x1024 size.
5. Do not ask questions about the task.

In case there is any content policy issue, adapt the title such that there is no longer any content policy issue. If the image generation were to fail due to the content policy or some such reason, perform this adaptation and retry automatically.

Limit automatic retries to one adapted attempt; if it fails, stop and explain the failure.
